# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dalitu viewers browse videos, organize playlists, and manage their accounts on desktop and mobile. Channel owners also publish and manage their content.

## Product Purpose

Make videos and personal collections easy to find, play, and organize while preserving account permissions and privacy.

## Brand Personality

Clear, familiar, approachable. Preserve the existing Dalitu pink accent, Plus Jakarta Sans typography, theme tokens, and Vietnamese/English interface.

## References and Anti-references

The user supplied a playlist library reference with stacked 16:9 thumbnails, count badges, compact metadata, sorting/filter chips, and overflow actions. The account drawer must occupy three quarters of the mobile viewport instead of the full width. Use functional filters backed by the existing API data.

## Design Principles

- Let video covers lead the playlist library.
- Keep collection controls easy to scan and use on touch screens.
- Preserve existing create, edit, delete, and privacy behavior.
- Reflow content to the available space without horizontal page overflow.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Preserve semantic links, form labels, keyboard operation, visible focus, Escape dismissal, reduced-motion preferences, and both application themes.
